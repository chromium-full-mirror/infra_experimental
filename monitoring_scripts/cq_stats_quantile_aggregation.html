This page is an experiment in aggregating consecutive CQStats quantiles from chromium-cq-status.appspot.com into an approximation of the quantiles over a larger interval of time.

This aggregation algorithm is unfortunately not suitable for use as it can have over 20% divergence from exact quantile values. Additionally it does not apply on a fundamental level to statistics that analyse attempts outside of the designated interval range like patchset-total-wall-time-durations.
